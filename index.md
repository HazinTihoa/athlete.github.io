## Overview

ATHLETE explores learning dynamic humanoid tennis skills from reusable locomotion datasets and video demonstrations.

## Interactive Demo

[**Launch Rally Demo →**](./rally/)

Try the trained humanoid tennis policy directly in your browser. Serve forehand or backhand balls, drag the ball to apply a physical perturbation, and observe the robot's response.

- **No installation or server GPU required.** Physics and policy inference run on your device.
- Use **B** to bring the ball nearby; drag it, or hold **Ctrl** and drag on the court. Release to let it fly.
- Ball speed is limited to **8 m/s** for interaction. Desktop Chrome or Edge is recommended.
- The first visit downloads the physics engine, robot assets and policy; allow a moment for loading.
